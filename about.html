Hi, I'm Eric!

I'm a Product Designer who cares about the intention behind every design. I love taking something complex and shaping it into an experience that feels clear and thoughtful.

I recently graduated from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville with a degree in Information Sciences, and I'll soon be heading back for my Master's.

My approach is built on empathy, research, and intent. I like to start by listening to the people I'm designing for and let their needs guide the decisions that follow. Most recently at UTK Innovation Labs, I did exactly that: I talked with students, faculty, and past clients, and turned those insights into a full website redesign that helped the lab connect with new business clients.

If you'd like to hear more about my process or the stories behind my work, please reach out. I'd love to chat.
